What's new

ChalkBored 1.1

  • Chalk Music: a new tab for your songs and playlists. Find a song, or add a link to one song or a whole playlist.
  • New looks: Basic (Night or Mono) and Space, with softer colors that are easy on the eyes. The old Terminal look is retired.
  • Chalk Chat and Chalk Browser open right in Notebook, like Chalk TV. You can switch back to a new tab in Settings.
  • More room on the Chalk TV, Music, Chat and Browser tabs.
  • Notebook opens faster, especially on slower laptops.
  • Safer class chat: moderators can now remove accounts that break the rules.
  • More lessons, and sharper versions of some.
